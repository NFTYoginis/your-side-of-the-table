## What this is

A set of markdown files that help a licensed residential real-estate agent **prepare their own side of
five conversations**. It is educational and preparatory material. It is not software, not a service,
and not a professional in any field.

## What this is not

**This is not legal advice**, and nothing in it may be used as a substitute for a licensed attorney.
The repository refuses to interpret, draft, or alter contract language by design — see
[`reference/the-upl-line.md`](reference/the-upl-line.md) — and that refusal is the correct behaviour,
not a limitation to work around. Interpreting a contract for a client is the unauthorized practice of
law in every U.S. state regardless of your real-estate licence.

**This is not tax, financial, or insurance advice.** Loan-program limits on seller-paid costs, the tax
treatment of concessions, and insurance implications of inspection findings are all outside its scope
and route to your lender, your accountant, and your carrier respectively.

**This is not compliance guidance.** Your obligations come from your state real-estate commission,
your brokerage, your MLS, and your local association. Where anything in this repository conflicts with
any of them, **they win, every time.** This repository takes the strictest available reading of the
UPL boundary, which is safe but is not tailored to your state, and safe is not the same as correct
for your situation.

**This is not a source of market data.** It holds no MLS, IDX, or aggregator data, and it will not
estimate a local rate, comp, price, or repair cost. See
[`reference/no-listing-data.md`](reference/no-listing-data.md). The national figures in
[`reference/compensation-landscape.md`](reference/compensation-landscape.md) are dated secondary
aggregates with their sources and their disagreements shown; they are context, never an answer, and
never a "standard."

## About AI-generated output

These files are designed to be loaded into an AI assistant. That has consequences you own:

- **The assistant can be wrong.** It can misapply a rule, invent a detail, or produce a confident
  answer to a question it should have refused. The instructions here reduce that; nothing eliminates it.
- **Everything it produces is a draft for your judgment.** You are the licensed professional. The
  output is prep, and prep is not a decision.
- **Check anything before you say it to a client.** Particularly a number, particularly a claim about
  what a document does.
- **Your E&O policy may not cover AI-assisted work.** The operator's build brief for this repository
  reports that 2026 errors-and-omissions carriers have begun adding generative-AI exclusions. That
  report is **not independently verified here.** Ask your carrier directly whether your policy
  excludes claims arising from generative-AI-assisted work — a call worth making regardless.
