## Rules for this file

- **First-hand only.** If you didn't see it, don't write it. A guess recorded here becomes a number
  the tool quotes back to you with confidence.
- **Date everything.** Compensation behaviour has moved meaningfully since 2024 and will keep moving.
  An undated observation is unusable within two quarters.
- **Ranges, not points.** "2–2.5%, mostly at 2.5% under $600k" is far more useful than "2.4%".
- **No client names, addresses, or financials.** Patterns, not deals. [`../../SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md).
