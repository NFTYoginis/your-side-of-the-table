# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be decent. This is a small repository about a subject — what agents charge and
how they defend it — where people hold strong views and some of those views are about each other's
livelihoods.

**Expected:**

- Disagree about the substance. "That objection response would get eaten alive in my market, here's
  why" is exactly the contribution this repo wants.
- Bring what actually happened, including when it went badly.
- Assume the person on the other side of an issue is a working professional with different conditions,
  not someone who hasn't thought about it.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone's competence, fee, or business model
- Naming and criticizing a real agent, brokerage, or client
- Using an issue thread to advertise a service, recruit, or generate leads
- Sustained argument after a maintainer decision, particularly on the refusals in
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) — those are settled, and the reasoning is written down

## On fee disagreements specifically

People argue about commission with unusual heat. Two ground rules keep it useful:

1. **Nobody's number is wrong here.** This repo helps agents state and defend *their own* fee. It has
   no view about what that fee should be and won't develop one.
2. **Don't propose a rate as standard, customary, or what everyone should charge** — not in an issue,
   not in a PR, not in a comment. Beyond being outside this repo's scope, representing compensation as
   standard is the conduct the 2024 practice changes came out of.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space in this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that don't meet this standard, and may block
repeat offenders. Report a problem by opening an issue, or by contacting a maintainer directly if the
report itself shouldn't be public. Reports are handled discreetly.
